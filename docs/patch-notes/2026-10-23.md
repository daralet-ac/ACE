# Daralet Patch Notes: October 15 – 23, 2026

This patch rebuilds how power scales from tier to tier, makes armor penetration and crits hit harder, shows what your loot rolled when you appraise it, adds a Looking for Group system, and starts timing capstone runs for a new leaderboard.

---

## Tier Power Rework

We rebuilt how gear and monsters scale across tiers so that each tier is a clear step up.

**Loot and items**
- Weapon damage and player armor and ward now **double every tier**.
- Armor and ward roll between **1x and 2x** of their tier's base value. Shields keep their old armor ranges.
- Melee and thrown weapon damage rolls from your weapon's own speed and variance. The range runs from the tier's target damage up to double it. Quest weapons and Upgrade Kits use the same range.
- Bows, crossbows, atlatls and casters roll from 1x to 2x multiplier ranges. Restoration bonuses follow the war and Nether ranges.
- Ammo max damage is now set by tier, and jewelry ward doubles every tier.
- Workmanship still grades rolls on the old scale.
- **Ward tinkers** now add a percentage of the item's base ward:
  - Silver: +5% ward
  - Pyreal: +3% ward and +3% armor
  - White Jade: +3% ward
- **Your existing gear is converted to the new scale.** Each item keeps where its roll sat in the old range and lands at the same place in the new one. Tinkers keep their share. Rolled quest weapons re-roll from their stored roll quality.
- Weapons whose base speed changed (two-handers, Warden's Greatsword, thrown loot) take the new speed. They keep their own speed roll and tinkers.

**Monsters**
- Monster health now follows a smooth curve from 20 to 3,000, and their armor and ward are tuned so kill times stay on target.
- Monster damage is tuned against a typical player's armor and ward for their level.
- Fellowship dungeons now scale everyone to the same tier 6 targets.

**Magic**
- Life magic damage only gets the elemental damage bonus from Nether casters.
- Each active Void damage-over-time effect on a target makes it take **5% more direct damage**.
- Weapon bleeds scale with your weapon's damage.
- Ward rending and ward penetration now apply to damage-over-time ticks.

**Arena**
- Duel damage now takes the difference in weapon tiers into account.

## Armor and Ward Penetration

Penetration now **adds damage**, and it adds more against heavily armored targets. Before, it ignored a fixed share of armor.

- Armor Cleaving, Ward Cleaving, Armor Rending, Ward Rending and the Spear and Orb specialization bonuses now add up into **one penetration value**.
- Bonus damage = penetration × 0.75 × (target's armor ÷ the average armor for its level), capped at 1.3x the average.
  - An average loot cleave (13.3%) adds about **10% damage** against average armor at every tier.
  - Against heavily armored targets, the bonus grows up to 30% larger.
- The bonus is based on the target's base armor. Debuffs like Imperil don't weaken it.
- The Ward Penetration rating still lowers ward by a flat share.
- Appraising an item now shows how much damage its penetration adds against average and heavily armored targets.

## Critical Hit Rebalance

- **Critical Strike now stacks with Biting Strike**, and **Crippling Blow now stacks with Crushing Blow**. Before, only the higher of each pair applied.
- **Crushing Blow** is now worth **1.3x** its listed value. This applies to all items, old and new, and appraisal shows the new value.
- **Crippling Blow** now rolls **65–130%** (was 50–100%).
- The **Mace and Staff specialization** bonus is now **+65%** (was +50%).

---

## Weapon Variance

- **Maces and staves** now have **0.6** damage variance (was 0.75).
- **Thrown axes, clubs, daggers and shuriken** now have **0.6** variance (was 0.75), matching axes and daggers.
- Existing weapons are converted so their damage keeps its place in the new range. Their loot roll and Granite tinkers carry over.

## See Your Loot Rolls on Appraisal

Loot weapons, armor and jewelry now show a **Roll Ranges** section when you appraise them, the same way quest gear does. It lists each rolled stat's range at the item's tier and where your item landed, for example:

> ~ Damage: 120 - 240 (63% roll)

The section covers:
- Weapon damage, launcher damage modifier, and caster elemental damage and restoration bonuses
- Attack, defense and skill mods, plus the weapon-type bonus (Biting Strike, Crushing Blow, Stamina Cost Reduction, Armor and Ward Cleaving)
- Armor level and ward level (tier 2 and up), armor skill and vital mods, and armor gear ratings (tier 6 and up)
- Jewelry ward level and its necklace, ring or bracelet rating

If an item has been tinkered, the header reads **"Roll Ranges (before tinkering)"**.

*Known limits:* an item that has been both upgraded and tinkered may show a less accurate roll. A caster staff's defense mods are shown as one range combined with its staff defense bonus.

## Looking for Group

You can now find a group, or find more players for your fellowship, without a plugin.

**Looking for a group**
- `/lfgroup <activity>`: look for a group. You'll join the first fellowship that is looking for more, as soon as there is one.
- `/lfgroup join <name>`: join the fellowship that player is in (or whose post you saw).
- `/lfgroup list`: see who is looking for a group.
- `/lfgroup stop`: stop looking.

**Looking for more (fellowship leaders)**
- `/lfmore <activity>`: players looking for the same thing join your fellowship automatically.
- `/lfmore <activity> manual`: you get a yes/no prompt for each player instead.
- `/lfmore invite <name>`: invite a player (same as `/invite`).
- `/lfmore list`: see which fellowships are looking for more.
- `/lfmore stop`: stop looking.

**Activities you can use**
- `dungeon` or `capstone`: any capstone you're high enough level for. Add a level (`dungeon 30`) or a difficulty (`d5`, `d5-10`) to narrow it down.
- A capstone by name or short name, with an optional difficulty: `gmg d10`, `gmg d10+`, `hoth`, `mod`...
- `leveling`: only fellowships where XP would be shared evenly with you.
- `quest` or `quest <town>`
- Anything else is matched by its words. For example, `olthoi` matches `olthoi north`.

**In the LFG channel**
Each new request is posted in the LFG channel with the command to answer it:

> LFM Green Mire Grave d10 (3/9, levels 30-35) - to join: /lfgroup join Arwen
> LFG Green Mire Grave d10 (level 34) - to invite: /lfmore invite Holmez

To keep the channel readable, the same request is posted at most once every 5 minutes, and your posts are limited to one a minute. Your request still updates and matches right away. Only the post waits.

**Good to know**
- Your request lasts until you stop it, join a fellowship, or log out. A fellowship's request lasts until it is full, locked or disbanded, and stays in place if the leader changes.
- Players who have squelched each other aren't matched, and neither is anyone in the arena.
- The commands are `/lfgroup` and `/lfmore` because the game client keeps `/lfg`, `/group` and `/fellow` for its own chat channels.

## Capstones

- **Capstone leaderboard:** capstone runs are now timed. The clock starts when your fellowship's copy of the dungeon is created and **stops when the boss dies**. Fastest times appear on the new capstone leaderboard on the website, next to a new **arena leaderboard**.
- **Starting a capstone run now locks your fellowship**, so nobody new can join partway through. Members who leave can still be invited back for a while.
- **The Shattered Source** is now an instanced capstone. Each fellowship gets its own private copy of the residence and the boss room, and you need a fellowship to enter.
- Staff can now remove characters from the capstone leaderboard.

---

## Arena

- `/arena` help has been reorganized into clear sections (Finding a duel, Duel options, Watching and rankings), with one command per line. Your current arena status now shows at the bottom.

## Daralet Player Plugin

The server now supports the new **Daralet player plugin** for Decal. With it you get:

- **Bank and loot windows** that replace the game's own windows for banks, corpses and chests.
- **Loot rules and filters** by weapon type, the item's special property, and roll ranges at each tier, plus a **"Sellable only"** option that skips items the Market Broker won't take.
- **Compare to worn:** compare items against what you have equipped. Item descriptions include roll quality and weapon speed.
- **Character tab:** your full Combat Focus character stats sheet (gear mods, ratings, ward, armor level, resistances, jewel effects).
- **Quests tab:** your contracts with descriptions, start and end NPCs, map locations, stage, solves and timers. You can also abandon contracts here; starter contracts still can't be abandoned until they're done.
- **Market tab**, and a **Find a group** tab built on the new Looking for Group system, with `[Join]` and `[Invite]` links on LFG channel posts.
- **Inventory improvements:**
  - Rings and bracelets go into the slot you drop them on, including the left wrist and hand.
  - A shirt and pants no longer take each other off.
  - Dropping an item onto an open vendor's panel adds it to the sell list instead of dropping it on the ground.

Related fixes:
- Plugin messages no longer show up as clutter in your chat window, including when another chat plugin such as Virindi Chat System is running.
- Unique items bought from a vendor now show up in plugins right away instead of after you relog.
- The Market tab no longer slows down the server while it's open.

## Bug Fixes

- **Fixed the game patch sometimes ending before all files were delivered.** This could crash characters on entering the world, especially in changed areas or when carrying items that use new art, and could take several launches to finish patching.
- Fixed some monsters getting too much mana for their level, and fixed a case where a monster's health, stamina or mana could be calculated wrongly.
